3. Third-party AI: what is shared and why

We use Google Gemini API, provided by Google, to extract recipe information and apply requested changes. Requests pass through our Supabase-hosted backend before being sent to Google.

ActionContent sent for processing
Photo recognitionOnly photos you select or capture for recognition, plus an extraction instruction and language setting. The app re-encodes these images to remove original location metadata. Private information visible inside an image can still be included.
Link import requiring AIThe recipe URL, extracted webpage text and language setting. Some structured recipe pages can be parsed without AI. The website and its image hosts receive ordinary network requests when the app loads their content.
AI recipe editingThe current recipe fields, your change request, up to eight recent successful edit instructions for context, and language setting. Stored recipe photos are not automatically attached to text-edit requests.

We do not include your Apple transaction, subscription verification, App Attest proof or support email in Gemini prompts. We do not send your whole photo library or entire saved cookbook to Gemini.

4. Your AI choice

Before the first AI request, the app identifies Google Gemini and Supabase, explains the content sent, and offers Allow AI processing and Not now. Opening the app, paying for Pro, or viewing this policy does not grant AI permission. Declining does not send the pending AI request. You may turn off AI data sharing in Settings; the next AI action asks again. Saved recipes and manual editing remain available. Turning sharing off stops future AI requests, but cannot recall a request already sent or automatically delete existing provider records.

6. AI training, storage and security

Our AI service is intended to operate using the Gemini paid API. Under Google's paid-service terms, prompts and responses are not used to improve Google's products. Google may nevertheless log prompts and responses for a limited period for safety, abuse prevention and legal requirements. We do not promise zero retention or immediate deletion by Google. Google's current terms govern its processing.

Our backend retains AI-generated result text associated with request IDs for retry handling, as well as service, usage and security records. The current implementation does not automatically expire these backend records. Original selected photo files are processed by the request handler and are not uploaded to a persistent photo-storage bucket by the app. Infrastructure logging and Google's processing are separate from your locally saved recipe.

We use HTTPS and server-side access controls. No system can guarantee absolute security. Avoid submitting sensitive personal information that is not needed for a recipe.
