Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 23, 2025  ·  Developer: Jing Gong  ·  buyu02596@gmail.com

1. Overview

JotBase ("the App") is a personal record-keeping application that uses AI to automatically organize your notes and entries. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information
When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your email address and display name (if provided by the sign-in provider). We do not receive your password.

2.2 User-Generated Content
We store the content you create within the App, including:

  • Text entries and notes you record
  • Images you attach to entries
  • Category names and organizational structure you create

2.3 Purchase Information
We collect information related to your subscription or in-app purchases through RevenueCat, including subscription status, purchase history, and remaining usage credits. We do not collect or store your payment card information. All payment processing is handled by Apple.

2.4 Usage Data
We may collect basic anonymized usage information such as app crashes and error logs to help improve the App.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • Location data — We do not access or collect GPS or location information
  • Contacts — We do not access your contact list
  • Browsing history — We do not track your activity outside the App
  • Camera data — Images are only accessed when you explicitly choose to attach a photo

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and sync your records across your devices
  • Process AI classification of your entries via Google's Gemini API
  • Manage your subscription and usage credits
  • Authenticate your identity and secure your account
  • Improve the App's performance and fix bugs

6. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored locally on your device using Apple's Core Data framework, and in the cloud via Supabase with servers located in the United States. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data.

7. Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your data will be permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days.

8. Your Rights

  • Access — View your data within the App at any time
  • Delete — Use "Delete Account" in Settings to permanently remove your data
  • Export — Contact us at buyu02596@gmail.com to request a data export

9. Children's Privacy

JotBase is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
